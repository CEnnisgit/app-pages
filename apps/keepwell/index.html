Clean up your camera roll, one month at a time.

Keepwell is an iPhone app for reviewing your photos. Swipe left to delete and right to keep. Short sessions, clear choices.

  • Month by month: pick a month, swipe through it, done.
  • Similar photos, your way: group look-alike shots and choose how similar counts.
  • Safe by design: photos and videos only. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.
  • Private: everything runs on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking.
