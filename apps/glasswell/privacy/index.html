Privacy Policy: Glasswell

Effective date: October 2026. This policy covers the Glasswell iOS app (including its widgets and Apple Watch app), published by Nimbus Web Design LLC ("we", "us").

Summary: Glasswell doesn't collect any data. Everything you log stays on your device. We have no account system, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Data we collect

None. Glasswell doesn't collect, store, transmit, sell or share personal data or usage data. That's why the App Store privacy label for Glasswell reads "Data Not Collected".

What stays on your device

Your drink log, daily goal, cup sizes and reminder settings are stored only on your iPhone and Apple Watch. Glasswell has no server, and it doesn't sync this data through iCloud or any other service. Deleting the app removes all of it.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

Connecting to Apple Health is optional. If you turn it on, iOS shows its standard permission screen, and you choose exactly what Glasswell may do:

  • Write: Glasswell saves the water you log to Apple Health.
  • Read: Glasswell reads back the water it saved, so your history in the app matches Apple Health.

Glasswell asks for water only. It doesn't request any other Health data type.

The water data Glasswell reads back is used only to show your totals in the app, and only on your device. We never receive it. Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with anyone. Glasswell doesn't store Health data in iCloud.

You can change or revoke access at any time in the Health app (Profile > Apps > Glasswell) or in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health. Glasswell keeps working without Health access. Data Glasswell has already written to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app. Apple's handling of Health data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders and notifications

If you allow notifications, Glasswell schedules drink reminders on your device using iOS local notifications. No push server is involved, and nothing about your reminders leaves your device. You can change reminder times in the app, or turn notifications off in Settings > Notifications > Glasswell.

Purchases

Glasswell Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple processes all payments (StoreKit). We never receive your name, email address, payment card or billing details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so Pro features can be unlocked and restored. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Analytics, advertising and tracking

Glasswell contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. It shows no ads and doesn't track you across apps or websites. If you opt in to sharing diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymized crash reports under Apple's own terms. We use those only to fix bugs.

Children's privacy

Glasswell is rated 4+ and doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a child's behalf. Parents can remove everything by deleting the app, and can delete any Health entries in the Health app.

Your choices

  • Revoke Apple Health access in the Health app at any time.
  • Turn off notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Delete your log in the app (Settings > Delete All Data), or delete the app to remove everything Glasswell stored on your device.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Glasswell handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future version collects any data, we'll update the App Store privacy label too.
