Keepwell Support

Keepwell helps you clean up your iPhone camera roll one month at a time. Swipe left to delete and right to keep, group look-alike shots with a similarity slider you control, and see how much space each session freed. Everything runs on your device.

Frequently asked questions

I deleted a photo by mistake. Can I get it back?

Yes, for 30 days. Every photo or video you delete in Keepwell goes to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app. Open Photos, go to Collections, scroll to Utilities > Recently Deleted, unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID, select the items, then tap Recover. Before you confirm a deletion, Keepwell's Undo reverses your recent swipe choices. After 30 days, iOS removes them permanently and they can't be recovered. Apple's guide: Delete photos on your iPhone or iPad.

What does the free version include?

Unlimited swiping and keeping, plus 50 deletions per day. The count resets at midnight, your local time. Keepwell Pro removes the daily deletion limit. Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Keepwell Pro, open Keepwell, go to the Keepwell Pro screen, and tap Restore Purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Keepwell and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Step-by-step: Cancel a subscription from Apple. For refunds, see Request a refund from Apple. The lifetime purchase is one-time and never renews.

Does Keepwell upload my photos?

No. Photos and videos are analyzed only on your device using Apple's frameworks. Keepwell has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the Privacy Policy.

Does Keepwell touch my contacts or other data?

No. Keepwell only works with photos and videos in your Photos library.

Keepwell can't see all my photos.

You may have given limited access. Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Photos > Keepwell and choose Full Access. Photos stored only in iCloud may need a network connection to load.
