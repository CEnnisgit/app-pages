# Privacy Policy: Quietwell

**Effective date:** October 2026. This policy covers the Quietwell iOS app, published by {{SELLER}} ("we", "us").

**Summary:** Quietwell doesn't collect any data. Every sound is generated live on your iPhone. We have no account system, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

## Data we collect
None. Quietwell doesn't collect, store, transmit, sell or share personal data or usage data. That's why the App Store privacy label for Quietwell reads "Data Not Collected".

## What stays on your device
Your saved mixes, sound settings and sleep timer preferences are stored only on your iPhone. Quietwell makes no network calls and doesn't stream or download audio: every sound is synthesized on the device. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Background audio
Quietwell uses iOS background audio only to keep your chosen sound playing while the screen is off or locked, and to show play and pause controls on the Lock Screen, in Control Center and in Now Playing. It doesn't use the microphone and doesn't record anything.

## Purchases
Quietwell Pro is sold through the App Store, and Apple processes all payments (StoreKit). We never receive your name, email address, payment card or billing details. Apple tells the app only whether a purchase is active, so Pro features can be unlocked and restored. Apple's handling of purchase data is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

## Analytics, advertising and tracking
Quietwell contains no analytics, advertising or third-party SDKs. It shows no ads and doesn't track you across apps or websites. If you opt in to sharing diagnostics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymized crash reports under Apple's own terms. We use those only to fix bugs.

## Children's privacy
Quietwell doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Because we collect no data, there's nothing for us to access, correct or delete on a child's behalf. Parents can remove everything Quietwell stored by deleting the app.

## Your choices
Delete the app to remove everything Quietwell stored on your device.

## Changes to this policy
If we change how Quietwell handles data, we'll update this page and its effective date before the change takes effect. If a future version collects any data, we'll update the App Store privacy label too.
