# Quietwell Support

Quietwell is a simple sound machine for sleep, naps, focus and quiet time. Every sound is generated live on your iPhone, so it never loops with a gap, never needs a download and works in airplane mode.

## Frequently asked questions

### The sound stops when I lock my phone.
Make sure the sound is playing in Quietwell before you lock the phone, and that a sleep timer isn't set shorter than you expect. If another app starts playing audio, iOS may pause Quietwell. Start it again from the Lock Screen or Control Center.

### How does the sleep timer work?
Tap the timer button, pick 15 minutes to 8 hours (or play all night) and tap Start Timer. When the timer ends, the sound fades out slowly so it doesn't stop all at once.

### What does the free version include?
Five sounds (white, pink and brown noise, fan and rain) with tone and intensity controls, plus the sleep timer. There's no time limit, no ads and no account. Quietwell Pro adds mixing (layer up to four sounds), more generated sounds (ocean, thunder, green noise and a low hum), saved mixes and a wake-up fade. Pro is available as Monthly ($2.99) or Yearly ($12.99) auto-renewing subscriptions, or Lifetime ($19.99, one time). These are US prices. Your local price is shown in the app before you buy. There's no weekly plan.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Quietwell Pro, open Quietwell, go to Settings (or the Quietwell Pro screen) and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Quietwell and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Step-by-step: [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). For refunds, see [Request a refund from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). The lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase and never renews.

### Does Quietwell collect my data?
No. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

### Is Quietwell a medical device?
No. Quietwell plays sounds. It doesn't diagnose, treat or cure any condition.
