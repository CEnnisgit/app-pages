# Glasswell Support

Glasswell is a simple water tracker. Log a glass in one tap from the app, a widget or your Apple Watch, see how close you are to today's goal, and get reminders that stop once you reach it. Everything stays on your device.

## Frequently asked questions

### Why am I not getting reminders?
Check three things:
1. Notifications are allowed: Settings > Notifications > Glasswell > Allow Notifications.
2. Reminders are on in Glasswell (Settings > Reminders), and your waking hours are set correctly.
3. A Focus mode or Sleep schedule isn't silencing them.

Glasswell Pro's smart reminders stop for the day once you reach your goal, so no reminder after you hit it means they're working.

### How do I change Apple Health permissions?
Open the Health app, tap your profile picture, tap Apps, choose Glasswell, and turn reading or writing on or off. You can also go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Glasswell. Glasswell works without Health access. Your log is simply kept only in the app.

### My total doesn't match Apple Health.
Glasswell only counts water you log in Glasswell. Water logged by other apps or entered by hand in Health isn't added to your Glasswell total. Make sure Glasswell is allowed to read and write water in Health (see above). If you delete an entry in Glasswell, it's removed from Health too.

### How do I add the widget or use Glasswell on Apple Watch?
- **Home Screen:** touch and hold an empty area, tap Edit > Add Widget, search for Glasswell, pick a size, and tap Add Widget. Tap + on the widget to log a glass.
- **Lock Screen and Control Center (Pro):** touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, and add the Glasswell widget. For Control Center, open it, tap +, then Add a Control, and choose Glasswell.
- **Apple Watch (Pro):** open the Watch app on your iPhone, find Glasswell under Available Apps, and tap Install. You can also add the Glasswell complication to a watch face.

### What does the free version include?
One-tap logging, a daily goal, reminders, a Home Screen widget, Apple Health sync and 7 days of history. Glasswell Pro adds smart reminders that stop at your goal, custom drinks and cup sizes, Lock Screen, Control Center and Apple Watch logging, and full history. Pro is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy.

### How do I restore my purchase on a new phone?
Sign in with the same Apple Account you used to buy Glasswell Pro, open Glasswell, go to Settings > Glasswell Pro, and tap **Restore Purchases**.

### How do I cancel my subscription?
Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, choose Glasswell and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Step-by-step: [Cancel a subscription from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118428). For refunds, see [Request a refund from Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). The lifetime purchase is a one-time purchase and never renews.

### How do I delete my data?
Glasswell stores your log only on your device, so we hold nothing to delete. To clear it:
- In the app: Settings > Delete All Data.
- Or delete the app, which removes everything Glasswell stored.

Water Glasswell already saved to Apple Health stays in Health. To remove it, open Health > Browse > Nutrition > Water > Data Sources & Access, and delete Glasswell's entries.

### Does Glasswell collect my data?
No. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. See the [Privacy Policy](../privacy/).

### Is Glasswell medical advice?
No. Glasswell is for everyday tracking. Ask your doctor how much water is right for you.
